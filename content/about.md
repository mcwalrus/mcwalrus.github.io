I'm **Max Collier**, a software engineer based in Melbourne, Australia.

Originally I'm from Central Otago, New Zealand. Aotearoa has a long history of
backing bold ideas, and I believe our next chapter depends on giving the next
generation the room, education and resources to build something of their own.

I am currently a volunteer on the IT / web-team at [The Opportunity Party](https://www.opportunity.org.nz/)
as a small way to put my technical skills toward a cause I believe in - that
everyone deserves a real chance to make their way in the world. Feel free to
learn about the party by reading up on the [Party Policies](https://www.opportunity.org.nz/policy).

I spend a large chunk of my time learning across a breadth of new technologies.
The economic cost of R&D has _fundamentally inverted_ since the advent of AI.
What used to take team months may now takes focused builders days. I focus on
experimental, exploratory work and techniques of developing and working with AI.

I believe most people are still unaware of the true economics and useful
strategies for how to adopt AI tooling best (this includes myself!). Every
technological revolution lays the foundations for the next, and I want to
be a positive force in shaping what is yet to come. Ideally, as humans we will
still be in control of how we choose to adopt this technology.

My skill-set sits at the intersection of applying Data Science, Web Development
and Platform Engineering together - using Artificial Intelligence (AI) is only
the latest tool in the tool-box for me. My previous career experiences at
[Bodeker Scientific](https://www.bodekerscientific.com/), [TracPlus](https://www.tracplus.com/), [Starboard Maritime Intelligence](https://www.starboardintelligence.com/) and recently [Entain (ANZ)](https://entaingroup.com.au/), have progressed me with building scalable real-time analyst
systems, with a common theme of geospatial remote sensing analysis (GIS-related)
under-pinning my experience.

Primarily in our world, I believe we rely on good people, and the decisions they
make to affect positive change in society or business. AI is now our best tool for
making informed decisions of our own — and that's where I see the opportunity:
more good decisions, made faster, by more people. Let us hope we will use this
to our advantage in the 21st century.
